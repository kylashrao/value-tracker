About Us

Welcome to Value-Per-Dollar Tracker. Our mission is to provide consumers with absolute price transparency in an era of persistent inflation and deceptive package scaling (shrinkflation).

We believe consumers deserve to know exactly what they are paying for. Our platform strips away marketing gimmicks by calculating the true, unvarnished cost-per-unit metrics for everyday household groceries, bulk goods, and digital streaming entertainment networks.

Our database rankings are completely data-driven, objective, and unbiased. We do not accept corporate sponsorships to manipulate calculation verdicts or inflate product value scores.
