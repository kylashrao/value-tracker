💡 Master Unit Economics: How to Calculate Value-Per-Dollar

Inflation continuously distorts basic item packaging shelf costs. To find true financial purchase health, you must strip away promotional packaging numbers and calculate real metrics down to the absolute metric unit.

1. The Cost-Per-Ounce Trick

Retail stores consistently charge a premium for convenient small container structures. Divide the total sticker price by the weight value listed in fluid ounces (fl oz) or dry mass grams to spot dynamic price-gouging across brand shelves instantly.

2. Track Hidden Shrinkflation

Manufacturers routinely reduce product weights by 5% to 10% while keeping identical outer packaging profiles and prices. Running routine live lookup scans identifies hidden changes, saving you money at the register.
