Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how to calculate true cost-per-unit, beat shrinkflation, and optimize purchase decisions.

The Value-Per-Dollar Tracker calculates the true unit cost of items across various categories (groceries, streaming services, SaaS, etc.) to strip away promotional packaging inflation and show real value.
For physical items like groceries, unit cost equals total price divided by net volume/weight in fluid ounces or dry mass. For streaming services, unit cost is evaluated on flat monthly rate relative to average content hours streamed.
Shrinkflation occurs when manufacturers reduce product sizes by 5%–10% while keeping the shelf sticker price identical. Routine unit-cost checks reveal these stealth price increases instantly.
Items are categorized automatically based on standard threshold benchmarks: Excellent Value ($0.015/oz or lower), Good Value ($0.030/oz or lower), or Expensive when exceeding threshold limits.
